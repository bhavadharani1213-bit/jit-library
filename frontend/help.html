Frequently Asked Questions
Common queries to get you unstuck quickly.
How do I reserve a book?
Use the “➕ Reserve New” button in My Books section on the dashboard. Then fill in the required details on the reservation page.
What happens if a book is overdue?
You will receive app & email alerts: overdue alert 2 days before due date and subsequent reminders. Fines (if any) are listed in your profile under “Fines”.
How do I change my password?
Click on your profile icon in the top-right, choose “Change Password”, and follow the prompt.
How can I contact the librarian directly?
Phone and email are available in the Contact section above. For urgent issues, call during working hours.
